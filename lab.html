Diving Under the line

Following the publication of the cover of *Stratégies* featuring an image I had created / generated using AI for the occasion, I wanted to bring this unusual diver to life: what on earth is this guy doing wearing that pink latex hat?

Kerosene

Kérosène is the problem every creative has but nobody's actually solved. The daily watch. That indispensable, time-eating ritual, LinkedIn here, Insta there, Creapills, Highsnobiety, La Réclame, Dazed — an hour lost every morning rebuilding the same thing from scratch. So I vibe coded the site I wished existed. No dev background. Just tools and instinct. Graphic design, Advertising, Architecture, Trends, AI, Photography — all in one place, every day, fresh. For me. For my clients. To never walk into a room not knowing what just dropped.
